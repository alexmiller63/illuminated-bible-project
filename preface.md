# Preface

To understand the heart of the Illuminated Bible, one need look no further than the illuminated Bibles of history. Those volumes used art to illuminate the Scriptures. This Illuminated Bible seeks to do the same, while extending that tradition by illuminating the text itself.

Alongside the English translation, the original Hebrew, Aramaic, and Greek, their transliterations, and carefully chosen notes invite the reader to see more clearly what the translators saw.

The title of this work is simply The Illuminated Bible. It is not intended to replace existing English translations, but to illuminate them by bringing the reader closer to the original languages while honoring the long tradition of illuminated manuscripts.
